# Code of conduct

Discours is built around open participation and serious disagreement without harassment. Project spaces should remain useful to people with different backgrounds, identities, disciplines, and levels of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique ideas and code precisely; do not attack people.
- Give context, evidence, and reproducible examples.
- Respect privacy, consent, and editorial safety.
- Make room for newcomers and communicate review decisions clearly.
- Stop behaviour that a maintainer identifies as disruptive or unsafe.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, sexualised attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

For ordinary moderation, flag the relevant issue or pull request to a maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). For sensitive reports, follow the private-channel procedure in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not post details publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate, documented where safe, and focused on protecting the community and the work.
